| 1 | What do supervisors check first, and at which level (ASN, order, line, carton)? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | Inbound: shipment reference, ASN and line-level details. | 2 | Is there a shared key linking Normal-flow receipts to outbound cartons? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | No other shared key exists; Normal-flow views can be kept separate. (A2) For Normal receipts the inbound LPN is mostly blank and, when present, looks nothing like the 13-digit Carton ID. SKU and Vendor Lot appear in both reports, so an approximate link is possible but cannot prove a receipt left in a given carton. (29 Oct) | Inbound and outbound appear as separate views; end-to-end trace only for Milkrun. If we show a SKU + Vendor Lot link, label it as approximate and log it as an assumption. |
| 3 | Is inbound LPN = outbound Carton ID for Milkrun intentional? | Answered | Asked · answered by course, not DSV | Yes. (29 Oct) | LPN to Carton ID is the Milkrun trace key. Apply the +8 h adjustment to inbound GR time in every comparison. |
| 4 | Is the Cyclone time the sorter scan, and does every carton pass through it? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | The glossary defines it as the time the carton is received into the Cyclone automation or sorting process. In a partial check, only Milkrun cartons carry it; Normal cartons carry a Packing Confirmation time instead. (29 Oct) For Milkrun, goods are received into Cyclone first and the admin then creates the order. (A3) Cyclone applies only to Milkrun cartons from Level 3; not every carton passes through it. (11 Nov) | Cyclone is the receipt-into-sorter milestone for Milkrun; Packing Confirmation is the matching milestone for Normal. Don't flag a missing Cyclone time on Normal flow. |
| 5 | What kind of diagnosis would be most useful? | Partly answered | Asked · answered by course, not DSV | Choose the diagnosis within the mandatory scope: planned-date and quantity reconciliation, and Milkrun traceability. Cycle-time, bottleneck and exception analysis are out of scope. DSV's preference was requested. (29 Oct) | Diagnosis covers reconciliation (dates, quantities) and traceability gaps only. |
| 6 | Which KPIs matter, and what does "late" mean? | Partly answered | Asked · partly answered by DSV | Key KPIs: order release status, quantity discrepancies and shipment readiness; investigate when there is a discrepancy or an order risks missing its required shipment timing. Don't use the Planned Post Date alone to judge lateness: release can be delayed on purpose, e.g. a customer hold or a fully booked flight. (11 Nov) | Show "released after the planned date" as a signal to review, not proof of lateness. Next (Q27): ask DSV for the required shipment timing and whether holds or flight changes are recorded. |
| 7 | Why do 62% of orders ship with a different forwarder from the default? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | It is normal for the actual forwarder to differ from the default; not an exception needing review. (A6) | No alert on forwarder changes; show as information only. |
| 8 | Why do 181 orders have a Planned Post Date before the order was created? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | May be due to an urgent order requirement. (A4) | Tag these as urgent orders, not data errors. |
| 9 | Are the 109 lines at exactly 2x duplicates or real over-receipts? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | Neither: rows with the same ASN# and Ref Line# have different functional packing (e.g. ITR vs ITY) and are counted separately. (A5) Course note: keyed by ASN# + Ref Line# + Functional Packing, every line reconciles. On 29 Oct the course also found that no line is short. | Use the three-part line key. No over-receipt alerts for these lines. |
| 10 | Which single forecast would help most? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | A what-if on the inventory or operational profile: (a) if the share of non-AS putaway zone types changes, how much location capacity is needed; or (b) if inbound/outbound volume rises by X%, how many more people are needed, from a baseline of 20. (B9) Present it as a prototype, since the data covers about one week. (29 Oct) | Choose one for the proof of concept and label it a prototype. |
| 11 | Which actions can a supervisor take? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | We choose the actions and justify them, then test them at the mid-project consultation. (11 Nov) | Rule actions follow DSV's list; every action still needs a human approval. |
| 12 | What should trigger an alert, and who acts? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | We choose the alert rules and justify them. (11 Nov) | Rule owners follow DSV's alert owners; thresholds stay ours until DSV sets them. |
| 13 | How much reasoning must a recommendation show? | Answered | Asked · answered by course, not DSV | Recommendations must be explainable and rule-based, with the rule and the reason shown. (29 Oct) | Each recommendation shows the rule, the data that triggered it and the threshold. |
| 14 | Were the 2,305 unmatched Milkrun cartons received before the data window? | Answered | Asked · answered by DSV | The course agreed with our explanation: Milkrun inbound GR times start 16 Aug 18:21, which is 17 Aug 02:21 after the +8 h adjustment, and in a partial check every unmatched carton had a Cyclone time before that. | 16 | What do AIFTA, RMA, Stock Transfer and Scrap Order mean, and do they have different KPIs or lead times? | Partly answered | Asked · partly answered by DSV | AIFTA: non-GST goods, normal milestones.
