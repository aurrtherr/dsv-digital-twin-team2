## Industrial Project Midterm Guideline

Each team presents what it has achieved against its project brief, with a short background and problem introduction. Version date: 5 November 2026.

### Format

- **Time:** 8 minutes per team, strictly timed, plus 2–3 minutes of questions. All 13 teams present in one 2-hour class, which may run up to 30 minutes over. Email me if you need to leave early.
- **Feedback:** given after the session, not on the spot.
- **Slides:** one deck per team, as a link in the Team Midterm Presentation tab of the SCDD Industrial Projects Google Sheet, by the end of Thursday, Week 9 (the day before the presentation). The running order will be posted in the same tab.

### Before you prepare

- Re-read your project brief in the project folder (briefs are updated in place): check the version date and any "Clarifications – Round 1" section.
- Use the Core Scope (required) as your checklist. Report optional extensions separately.
- If a client answer is pending, state a working assumption and keep it easy to change.

### What to present

| Part | What to show |
|---|---|
| 1. Background and problem | The client, the problem, why it matters |
| 2. Scope and approach | Project scope and the framework of your method |
| 3. Progress so far | Findings and accomplishments, with the methods used and evidence |
| 4. Assumptions, open issues | Working assumptions, pending client answers, data issues |
| 5. Plan to the final | A week-by-week timeline and the main risks |

### Rubric

The midterm presentation is worth 15% of the course grade. All teams are graded on the same standard, whatever their size.

| Criterion (weight) | Strong | Adequate | Weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Background (10%) | Clear, well motivated | Loosely motivated | Unclear or generic |
| 2. Scope and approach (20%) | Well defined; sound method | Mostly clear; partly justified | Unclear or unsuitable |
| 3. Progress (35%) | Solid; clear evidence | Some, limited evidence | Little or no evidence |
| 4–5. Assumptions and plan (15%) | Clear; realistic plan | Partial; plan lacks detail | Missing or unrealistic |
| Presentation and Q&A (20%) | Clear, on time, sound answers | Mostly clear; minor issues | Hard to follow or overruns |
